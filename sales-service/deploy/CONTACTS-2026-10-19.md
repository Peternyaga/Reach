Prepared locally; not deployed. Pair with hosted plugin 0.4.11 and local plugin 0.1.2.

Use the existing backup-first `install-upgrade.sh` procedure in `UPGRADE.txt`. Pause the agent poller before upgrading. The additive migration creates `lead_contacts` and a `leads.contacts_initialized` marker, then copies existing phone/email/role without inventing a person's name or ownership. It retains the existing contact/opportunity anchor and all old fields. Re-running migration does not duplicate contacts or resurrect deleted ones. No authentication scopes change.

The selected primary route supplies legacy phone/email/contact_role, including email composition and task call links. A contact role such as Owner does not assign the lead salesperson. Selecting an email-only primary intentionally leaves legacy phone empty; routes belonging to different people are never combined. Existing email authorization still binds its original recipient, project, sender and lead version. No extra route is automatically contacted.

On the profile Overview, Contacts lists each name/department, role, direct/shared/unknown ownership, phone and email. Add/edit/remove operate on individual IDs. Changes retain before/after audit history and provenance. Only one primary is allowed. Removing primary clears the old phone/email; choose a replacement explicitly if appropriate. Old profile phone/email edits change primary only. Stale batches are rejected in full. Collectors retain existing sales-detail restrictions.
